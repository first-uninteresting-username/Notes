# Passive voice exercises

Change into passive

1. They gave him a golden watch.
2. Poverty drove them to crime.
3. They'll arrange everything in time.
4. Stress often causes heart attacks.
5. The police didn't find any trace of him.
6. They must not cut down these trees.
7. They were signing the contract when I entered.
8. Are they going to cancel the match?
9. When will he publish his new novel?
10. Where did you hide it?

11. He was given a golden watch (by them).
12. They were driven to crime by poverty.
13. Everything will be arranged in time (by them).
14. Heart attacks are often caused by stress.
15. No trace of him was found (by the police).
16. Those trees must not be cut down (by them).
17. When I entered, the contract was being signed (by them).
18. Is the match going to be cancelled (by them)?
19. When will his new novel be published?
20. Where was it hidden (by you)?
