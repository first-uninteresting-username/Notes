It's a common dillema among present-givers: Should you ask the person you are giving your present what they want to receive or try to surpise them. I, when I am the one receiving gifts, like to get gifts I said I wanted, even if that means explicitly asking for them.

My first reason for holding this opinion is practicality. When I get what I said I wanted, the buying proccess is easier for the giving person, I am unlikely to get a gift I would be unhappy with and even if that happens, I am the one to blame. People remember bad presents for much longer time than good presents, and when you ask for specific thing, the probablity of that falls massively.

Another reason I like to notify what I want is past experience. I've gotten bad gifts multiple times in the past, sometimes when the gift-giver said it was gonna be, citing, "the greatest gift you will ever get".

In the conclusion, I would like to say that I am not saying that you should never get gifts you didn't ask for, but if you do, you should be prepared for the possibility of getting a bad gift. I personally will ask for specific gifts on all occasions, unless I can not.
